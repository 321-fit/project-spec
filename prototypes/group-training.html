Coach
Create Group Session
Coach creates a new training template. Key difference from personal — the Personal / Group toggle and Max participants field.
  • Training type selected via selection chips (standard pattern)
  • Stepper for participant limit — unified input with ± buttons
  • Price is set per participant
  • Payment — two toggleable chips Cash / Card (both can be selected)
Saved as training_session with is_group flag + max_participants
Coach
My Training Sessions
List of all coach's training templates. Group and personal sessions are visually distinguished by badges.
  • Personal badge — gray, neutral
  • Group badge — cyan/teal, shows max participants
  • Group pricing displayed as "€25/person"
  • "+" button to create a new template
Coach
Coach Calendar
24-hour vertical calendar. Group and personal events are visually distinct by border color.
  • Day strip-wheel — horizontal scroll, current day centered
  • Group events — cyan border + "7/10" badge
  • Personal — green border + athlete name
  • Current time — teal line with dot
  • Drag & drop — move events, red zones for occupied slots
  • Today / Sync buttons in header
Coach
Group Event Detail
Details of a specific group event. "Ticket card" layout — all key info in one compact block, participants right below.
  • Ticket card: badge + price, date/time, location, spots bar
  • Participant list — avatar + name + sport
  • Swipe left to remove a participant
  • Long press — bottom sheet (View Profile, Send Message, Remove)
  • X button on the right — quick remove
  • Cancel Training — red button at the bottom
Coach
Cash Payment Tracking
Screen for marking cash payments after a training session. Three interaction methods for maximum speed.
  • Tap on row — toggle checkbox paid/unpaid
  • Swipe right — green "Paid" reveal, marks as paid
  • Swipe left — red "Remove", removes participant
  • Long press — bottom sheet with full options
  • "Paid: 2/5" counter updates in real-time
  • Paid participants — dimmed style (gray name, semi-transparent avatar)
Unpaid status does not block completing the training for others
Athlete
Coach Profile
Coach profile from the athlete's perspective. Training sessions are immediately visible — no scrolling needed. About section at the bottom.
  • Avatar → name → location → sport chips
  • Group sessions with badges and spots counter
  • Full badge for fully booked trainings
  • About / bio at the bottom with "See more" — secondary info
Athlete's primary goal = book a session, not read the bio
Athlete
Group Training Detail
Group training details before joining. Ticket card with coach avatar, clickable participant list.
  • Ticket card: coach (avatar + name), date/time, location, spots bar
  • Tap on participant → bottom sheet (View Profile, Send Message)
  • Chevron ">" as a hint that the row is tappable
  • Join Training · €25 — sticky CTA at the bottom
Open registration — no approval needed, first come first served
Athlete
Group Training — Joined
What the athlete sees after joining a training. They appear first in the list with a "You" label.
  • Green "Joined" badge in the ticket card
  • Current user — first in list, gradient avatar + "You" label
  • Other participants are tappable (profile sheet)
  • Leave Training — red button (refund within 24h)

Group Training — Full
All spots are taken. The athlete can see the training but cannot join — button is disabled.
  • Red "Full" badge + red progress bar (100%)
  • Spots: "15/15" in red
  • Participant list collapsed ("+12 more")
  • "Training is Full" button — gray, disabled
V1 has no waitlist — just "Full" and that's it
Athlete
Athlete Schedule
Athlete's schedule — same calendar as the coach but in light theme and without drag & drop.
  • Group events — cyan border + "Group" badge
  • Personal — green border + coach name
  • Day strip wheel + Today / Sync
  • Athletes cannot create events directly — only through booking
