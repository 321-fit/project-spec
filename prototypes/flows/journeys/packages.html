Session packages — end-to-end
A package is a prepaid pass (gym-membership model): the coach sells N sessions of a template at a discount, the athlete pays the full amount upfront (cash or card), then redeems visits one booking at a time. The feature spans six screens across both roles — this map links each live screen (no copies) in flow order.
References, not duplicates — every step opens the canonical screen, so this never drifts from the source. Use the browser back button to return here.
Prepaid, upfront
Full amount paid on purchase; coach paid immediately. Booking burns 1 credit — no charge at booking.
Cash or card
Card = credits active instantly. Cash = credits activate when the coach marks payment received.
No expiry
Unused sessions never lapse — keeps refunds simple and sidesteps gift-card expiry law.
One type per pack
A pack = N sessions of one template (personal / group / self-paced). Refund of unused = manual coach action.
Coach sets it up
1
Coach
Sell a session as a package
On the training-session template (Create or Edit) → Packages (optional) section. Add one or more tiers (e.g. 5 · €200 · €40/ea, 10 · €360 · €36/ea) — the per-session rate + discount vs the single price computes live. Expiry defaults to No expiry. Works on personal, group, and self-paced sessions.

Discover & buy a pack
Coach profile → Book training. A session that offers packs shows a teal “Save with a 5-pack · from €40/session” strip. Tap it → buy sheet: pick a tier, choose Cash or Card, confirm. Cash = “pay in person, unlocks when the coach confirms”; Card = ready immediately.

Activate a cash package
A cash pack shows “Awaiting cash payment · Unpaid” and its credits stay locked until the coach taps “Mark received” → snackbar “Payment received · pack activated”. This gate stops an athlete redeeming an unpaid cash pack. Card packs skip this — active on purchase.
