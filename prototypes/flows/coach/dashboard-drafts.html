Coach · draft · Home, placed

Home · ordered by weight

Anchor
Bet: the shipped Home (dashboard.html) already knows everything it needs to say; it says it as six labelled sections in the spec's order, five of them identical cards. Ordered by the coach's morning — who you are today → what you owe an answer to → what is next → the money → the day → the signals — and with each block in its own grammar, the same content is one screen instead of two.

What changed and why

  • The anchor. Client Detail is held together by a person at the top — avatar, name, one line — and a greeting alone did not do that for Home. The next person (default): the identity block is the athlete you are about to meet, in exactly Client Detail's grammar (big avatar, IN 45 MIN · 10:30 as the kicker, name, what); it replaces the Next-session card, tap → the drawer; quiet day → tomorrow's person, grey kicker; idle/ready → no person to show, so the empty card returns. The day widget (default): the ring widget itself takes the anchor slot — no greeting at all, the day sits in the widget's corner, and the widget is cut to the ring + earned / planned; the week bars, sentence and link go (the Money widget carries the week below). Order becomes widget → Next session → circles → Needs you — the hero is your day in numbers, Revolut-style. The date: calendar-style, the date as the anchor. None: greeting + card, the first cut. Greeting shrinks to one line when an anchor is on.
  • Four circles, fixed slots: Book · Self-paced · Time off · Ask AI — the things a coach does from Home rather than in a tab. Invite gave its slot to Self-paced (2026-09-15): inviting lives on Clients, while self-paced work — requests to build, clips to review — arrives every day and had no home but a chip. Time off is the second most frequent weekday errand; Ask AI is the assistant's proposed FAB-grade entry. ⏳ the set is a bet.
  • Needs you is chips, not five cards. Cash to collect (red) · requests (blue) · to review / self-paced (yellow) · invites waiting (grey). Each opens its list — Cash to collect (new, across clients), Inbox on the right tab, the review queue, the self-paced hub. Cost: the cards' second lines (“1 awaiting your reply over 24h”, “Tom overdue”) move to the destination; the chip carries the count. All caught up replaces the block with one line.
  • Next session is the same card as on Client Detail — face instead of type tile (here the person is the variable), no buttons, tap → the calendar's drawer. Quiet day: tomorrow, when-line grey. Idle / Ready: the empty card takes the slot with secondary buttons (Book a session; + Share profile for a new coach).
  • The day widget (default; Money only is the first cut). One reusable shape — a ring for the one thing with a target, three bars for the things with a denominator, one sentence, one link — the Nutrition-card grammar, with our content. Coach: the ring is sessions today (fills through the day, so Home is alive at 8:00 and at 18:00), money around it (earned / planned), the week in the bars (This week vs last, Cash collected of cash due, Hours booked of hours open — the one number that says “open more hours” or “invite” without saying it), the sentence is the insight, the link is Earnings. Athlete, same component: ring = sessions this week of planned, bars = balance / self-paced done / streak, sentence = “Next: Tennis with Mark · tomorrow 10:30”, link = Schedule. Idle: falls back to the money widget (a ring of 0/0 is a hole). Ready: none.
  • Today never disappears — the rest of the day after the next one; empty is a quiet row.
  • Signals are tiles (a review = star plate, new clients = faces), one tip at a time as an outlined dismissable card (a suggestion is not system messaging), and the bookability warning is a banner above the circles, non-dismissable, exactly where Client Detail puts its status banner.
  • Pre-approval states (New wizard, Under review, Rejected) are not redrawn here — they are a different screen (the wizard) and stay as in the canon file.
What it costs: chips carry less than cards did, so the “why” behind a count is one tap further; four circles are four bets on what a coach does from Home, and any of them can be wrong; the tinted canvas is still a proposal for the whole coach side.

Every screen reached from here is copied into this file (source named on each) so the flow walks in one place. Tinted canvas + white CTA is the default; the switch above flips every phone.

The global twin of Client Detail's Owed: every cash debt across clients, oldest first, one row per owing entity, the canon cash drawer per row (Mark as paid / Waive). A settle is per entity on the backend, never per client or per total — the list makes that honest. Settled rows drop to Settled; last one → hero €0.
