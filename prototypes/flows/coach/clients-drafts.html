Pane
Bet: the shipped list (clients.html) is one flat stack of identical rows carrying lifetime stats (12 sessions · €580); the things a coach opens the tab for — who owes, who went quiet, who is on this week — sit in badges or on the other pane. Same content, ordered: what you owe an answer to → who you see this week → everyone.
  • Needs you = the five smart groups as chips (€ owed · inactive 30d · packs running low · not in app · new this month). Tap opens the smart group's list, exactly as the Groups pane did — they are doors, not filters (decision 2026-09-15). The Groups pane (the other segment, same screen) keeps only the manual groups — faces lead, unread count on the row, Create a group as a dashed row.
  • Rows speak of time, not lifetime money. Next · Thu 10:00 · Tennis (teal) or Last · Apr 2 · 5 sessions; the badge stays for owed / CRM / new. Lifetime revenue lives on the client's screen.
  • This week = the people you see, in order of when; Everyone = A–Z, plain rows. Letter dividers and an index rail were tried and dropped (2026-09-15) — noise at this size; on native the platform index (UITableView / fast-scroller) can appear past ~50 clients without a design decision here.
  • Search under the segmented control (type to try); while searching the chips, This week and the rail hide and the A–Z list filters.
  • + stays the one action (create · import · invite to app · invite to training — the canon sheet). A list is not an object, so no circles.
  • Archived & Blocked stays a row at the bottom (Mail Trash pattern). Fresh coach = one card with two doors: Import contacts / Invite by link.
  • No wash in the plain look (no identity block here); tinted canvas as everywhere.
What it costs: two lists of the same people (This week + Everyone) — a person can appear twice; sorting by next session needs the next-session field on the list endpoint (today the list carries lifetime counts only).
