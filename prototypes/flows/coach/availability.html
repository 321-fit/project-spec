Available hours

Coach's weekly schedule — when athletes can book training sessions. Set once, applies indefinitely (or until edited). New accounts are seeded with Mon–Sun 07:00–22:00, so the screen opens populated rather than empty.

  • One row per day of the week (Mon–Sun)
  • Checkbox toggles active/inactive for the day
  • Active day shows 1+ time intervals (start · to · end)
  • Tap + to add another interval on same day (morning/evening split with break)
  • Tap − to remove an additional interval
  • Tap a time input → opens time picker (wheel, 15-min snap — chunk 2)
  • Timezone subtitle is read-only (set at account creation / Settings)
  • Footer CTA Save availability — primary brand filled button (per footer_patterns)
