Invite to 321Fit (global)

One global invite screen, reused from every entry point — Settings (Invite a coach / Invite a friend), My Coaches, Clients. The entry label adapts ("Invite a coach", "Invite clients"); the destination is always this screen. Role-agnostic: athlete = light, coach = dark (toggle in sidebar).

Why unified: invite-to-the-app is one relationship. The OneLink encodes who's inviting (attribution) so a single screen serves both "bring a coach onto 321Fit" and "bring clients/athletes onto 321Fit". Replaces 4 divergent invite surfaces (coach/invite-coach.html, athlete s-invite, settings placeholders, CRM import).

QR tab — your avatar + name + QR (AppsFlyer OneLink) + Copy link / Share (native sheet) + Scan a code (in-person connect, either side can show or scan).

Contacts tab — permission prompt → connect → contacts split into "On 321Fit" (already members) and "From your contacts" (per-row Invite → prefilled SMS with your OneLink, nothing sent automatically). Reuses the CRM contact-import primitive from Clients.

Invited tab — people who joined through your link (the old coach→coach "recent joined" list, generalised). Most-recent-first. Only joined shown; link-opens aren't trackable to a person. Pills/rewards deferred to Phase 2 per project_invite_a_coach.

Backend: GET /me/referral-link → {url, token}; GET /me/invites → joined list; contacts match endpoint (hash phone → on-app flag) for the Contacts tab; signup accepts referral_token. All additive.
