Athlete · #43

The schedule on the phone’s home screen

Bet: a widget answers three questions in a glance — what do I have today, what is next, when is my child’s training — so it shows sessions and nothing else. No greeting, no stats, no marketing. The app’s job is everything after the glance.

Balance (review 2026-09-16, Apple’s own widgets as the reference)

  • One big thing per widget, regular weight. The hour in the small is 36px regular (Battery’s “100%”, Reminders’ “9”); nothing else is large. The earlier cut had four bold sizes fighting — that was the glue.
  • Type scale = three steps + one label. 15 (row name, medium weight) · 13 (sub, regular) · 12–11 (right-column day, regular) · one 11px caps label in the accent. Weight carries hierarchy, not size.
  • Small = Apple Calendar. Caps label, big hour, then the session as the calendar’s own tinted tile (tint = type, stripe = type) — the same tile the schedule draws. “in 45 min” is gone: the label says Next, the hour is absolute, “today” sits next to it in 13px.
  • Medium = Apple Mail. Two rows, name/sub left, time/day right-aligned in regular weight, a hairline between; the next one’s time in the accent. No big numeral here — two rows do not need one.
  • Large = a lighter week strip (today as a filled circle, dots under days) + the same Mail rows grouped by day labels. No footer.
  • No badges, no money, no relative times, no date in the header.

Skin

Two candidates, switch above. Brand canvas = the rework’s tinted teal recipe: the widget is recognisably ours among a dozen white/black system widgets. System material = the OS surface (dark #1c1c1e / light white) — quieter, blends with Calendar/Weather. Dark/Light in the sidebar switches the wallpaper and the system skin; the brand canvas is always dark (it is the identity, not a theme).

Platform rules this obeys

  • WidgetKit: a timeline of snapshots, no scroll, no animation, tap only (widgetURL for the whole widget, Link per row). Corner radius and content margins (16pt) are the system’s — we do not draw them; the background goes through containerBackground (a gradient is allowed).
  • iOS 18 tinted icons re-render the widget in one hue (switch above): colour is gone, so type is also a shape — filled dot personal, ring group, diamond self-paced — and weight carries the hierarchy.
  • HIG: one idea per size, no branding beyond a small identification mark (the 14px 321), a redacted placeholder at first placement, both appearances.
  • Data via an App Group snapshot the app writes; the extension never calls the network with the app’s session.

Data

What the Calendar tab reads: the athlete’s upcoming events (confirmed only — requests are the app’s business). Refresh: timeline every 30 min + reload on push (confirmed / cancelled / rescheduled). No new endpoint.

Athlete · #43

Large · the week

  • A seven-day strip at the top: today highlighted, past days dimmed, dots under the day = sessions, in type colour (two dots = two sessions). Tap a day → the Calendar on that day.
  • Under it the same rows as medium, four to five of them, grouped by day; self-paced reads “any” for time.
  • No footer — the rows fill the frame to the end of the week.
Coach · #43

The coach’s day, same widget

  • Same three sizes, the coach’s nouns: rows lead with the athlete’s name (the coach knows their sport), group rows show 7/10.
  • Small: next session + “in 45 min”; when requests are waiting a red count sits in the footer (see States).
  • Medium footer = the day widget’s line from the Home rework: requests waiting · € planned this week. Tap → Inbox.
Coach · #43

Large · the coach’s week

  • Week strip with per-day dots; a full day shows two or three dots, that is the signal.
  • No footer; the list runs to the end of the week (Fri, Sat) so the frame is full.
Android · Glance

Same content, Material shape

  • Glance = Compose-like API over RemoteViews, so: system font (no Rubik — the rows here are in the system face on purpose), no custom shapes beyond what RemoteViews draws, 28dp system corner radius, cells not points (SizeMode.Responsive: 2×2 → small layout, 4×2 → medium, 4×4 → large).
  • Rows are ≥40dp (48dp target with the gap) — taller than iOS; the medium widget grows with its cell instead of clipping.
  • Dynamic colour (Material You): the system skin takes the wallpaper’s tonal palette, not our teal — so on Android the system skin is the natural default and the brand canvas the opt-in.
  • Type shape + dot, who-pill, taps — identical. Reload: updatePeriodMillis ≥ 30 min, WorkManager + FCM push → update(). A preview + description for the widget picker is mandatory.
iOS · lock screen

Accessory widgets — almost free

  • The same timeline feeds three lock-screen families: inline (one line above the clock: Boxing with Maris · 18:30), circular (Next 18:30 / Week 3), rectangular (label / name / time · place).
  • Monochrome by definition (the system renders them vibrant-white over the wallpaper) — which is why type has to be a shape, not a colour, everywhere.
  • Same taps: the event, or the Calendar. Also shows in StandBy and on the watch face via the same families.
States · #43

When there is nothing to show

  • Nothing planned — the widget says so and gives one door: Find a coach (athlete) / Book (coach). It must not look broken.
  • Free day, something later — “Free day · next: Thu 09:00”.
  • Requests waiting (coach) — a red count in the footer; tap → Inbox.
  • Signed out — one line, tap → sign in.
  • Long names — the row clips with an ellipsis; time, dot and the who-pill never do.
  • Loading — skeleton bars at first placement; after that the OS shows the last snapshot, never a spinner.

Open

  • Show pending requests on the athlete side (“awaiting Maria”)? Proposal: no — confirmed only; the widget is a promise, not a maybe.
  • Refresh budget: WidgetKit ~40–70 reloads/day — 30-min timeline + push reloads fit.
