What changes — the twelve places

Found by scanning every prototype file for elements that directly hold two or more <button>: 446 rows. Twelve of them change; the screen lists all ten and nothing else.

What does NOT change, and why it is most of them:

  • "Are you sure?" — ~40 rows. Every Disconnect / Cancel, every Cancel / Save editor sheet. One question, one answer, one escape: the words are the meaning, and a destructive confirmation is the last place to make someone decode a glyph. The biggest family in the app, and untouched.
  • Action sheets (Take photo / Choose from library / Remove / Cancel). Unrelated choices, each needing a sentence, and a list does not run out of horizontal room at five items.
  • Outcome pairs (Missed / Mark complete, Skip / Rate now). Not peers: one is the expected outcome and the other the exception. "Missed" as a glyph is a guess.
  • Single buttons that are genuinely single — Top up, Send code.

That list is the reason this module exists before the change does: a styling sweep that missed the distinction would have turned destructive confirmations into icons.

Corrected 2026-08-20: the client card sheet and the self-paced review sheet were listed here and are gone. I had put them in from memory rather than from the scan — both carry a single CTA, not a row of peers. Twelve became ten.

… and what it becomes

The rule: peer actions on an object take circles; a question with an escape takes words. Count does not decide it — two peers are still peers, which is why Awaiting becomes two circles while Disconnect / Cancel stays two words.

Fixed positions: left the refusal, centre Reschedule, right the expected answer. The coach reads Cancel · Reschedule · Confirm, the athlete Decline · Reschedule · Accept — same places, role wording.

Colour is rationed: exactly one gradient (the expected answer, under the thumb), at most one red (destructive, farthest from it). A third fill turns a row of actions into a row of warnings. Destructive is filled red with a white glyph — it must read as destructive before the icon is identified. White on #f05c5b measures 3.29: under the 4.5 a label needs, over the 3.0 a glyph needs, and the word sits underneath on the page background.

Ask AI is drawn but not built — it is the fourth circle, outline only, for when the assistant exists. Outline because asking is not a decision; a third fill would flatten the row.

In the kit: promoted out of this file into .fit-action-circle / .fit-action-circles in fit-ui.css, with FitActionCircle in design-tokens (SwiftUI + Compose) so the three platforms cannot drift.

The neutral plate takes --fit-surface-higher, one step above whatever it sits on — on surface-high it was invisible, which is how a circle with no circle got this far.
