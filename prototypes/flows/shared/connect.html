Connect (QR)

Lightweight connect utility — the Instagram-nametag pattern. Purely “we met in person, let’s connect on 321Fit”. Role-agnostic (athlete = light, coach = dark). No referral / reward content, no contacts import — those live on the coach Referral screen and in the Clients tab respectively.

My code — avatar + name + QR (AppsFlyer OneLink) + Copy link / Share (native drawer). Symmetric with Scan: either side can show or scan.

Scan — native camera viewport with framing corners; scans the other person’s QR → resolves OneLink → connect. The scanline + corners are a page-local visual (cn-vp-*); on device this is the platform camera.

Entry points

  • Profile (both roles) — “Show my code” / share row
  • Athlete My Coaches — “Invite a coach” (connect your real-life coach)
  • Coach Clients — “Add / connect” a client in person

Opened as a sheet or push from those surfaces; × dismisses.

Backend

GET /me/referral-link → { url, token } (same OneLink token used everywhere). Scan resolves the token on the public /i/<token> landing → connect. All additive.
