Settings

Reached from the Profile gear (top-right). Copied from the coach Settings structure (.set-card rows) and trimmed to athlete scope.

  • Profile — Edit personal info (avatar, name, gender, height, weight — no intro video / cover, athlete-only fields), Invite a friend (placeholder, referral on hold).
  • Training — Choose a sport (reuses sport-types grid), Calendar sync (reuses Google flow).
  • Payments — Balance → spending ledger (the athlete mirror of the coach Earnings screen).
  • Account — Account access (shared sign-in / security).

Diff vs coach: no Stripe Connect / payout rows (athlete pays, doesn’t receive); Earnings → Balance; Invite a Coach → Invite a friend; +Choose a sport surfaced here.

Log out: no confirmation → toast “Signed out” (reversible, no data loss). Personal data / Sport types / Calendar sync now open athlete screens (cloned from coach — personal-data drops intro video + cover image; sport-types drops custom-sport creation; calendar-sync same as coach).
