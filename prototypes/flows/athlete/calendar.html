Same calendar as coach, light theme. The event drawer reuses the coach’s unified state-aware sheet (data-event-state + canonical .fit-sheet-footer-variant + status pills) — accept/decline happens right on the calendar (no deep-link into notifications).

  • Drag-reschedule — own 1-1 sessions only (revised 2026-07-28; the earlier “no drag & drop” note is retired). The athlete drags a future personal session on the grid exactly like the coach does; the drop is a reschedule request, not a unilateral move — the event goes to Awaiting until the coach accepts, and the current slot stays booked meanwhile (PATCH /athlete/training-events/{id}/reschedule). Not draggable: group sessions (coach-owned — the coach drives group reschedules), external Google/Apple events, cross-role coach tiles, and anything in the past. Mid-drag the tile shows the coach’s busy zones so you can’t drop onto a slot they can’t take; an invalid target paints the tile .invalid and the snackbar names the reason. Matches prod iOS ScheduleViewModel.isDraggable; shipped on Android in epic 321fit_android_new#122.
  • Header — descriptor + status pill + a message icon (revised 2026-07-28; it used to sit in the Planned footer). Messaging the coach works in every state, so it belongs to the sheet chrome, not to one state's action row — same rule on the coach drawer, where the header also carries the ⋯ action hub.
  • Planned — confirmed session. Footer: Reschedule (sends a new time as a fresh request — coach must approve; current slot stays booked until then) + Cancel → cancel-with-refund sheet (free up to 24h, € back to balance; within 24h = policy warning).
  • Request (yellow pill) — coach scheduled this, you must respond. Footer: Decline / Accept inline. (Same action also lives in the Inbox “To reply” tab — calendar is the time-view shortcut, [[project_inbox_unification]].)
  • Awaiting (dashed yellow tile + outlined yellow pill — tentative, not actionable) — you requested, waiting on the coach. Shows an expiry note (“Expires in 22h — auto-cancelled & refunded”, the 48h window). Footer: Cancel request. Mirror of the Inbox “Waiting” tab.
  • Finished (faded) — footer Rate this session → star sheet. The written review is a separate follow-up notification the day after the first session (not here).
  • Missed (red pill) — no-show. Footer: Book again.
  • Coach-confirmed note — blue note on a Planned event when the coach proxied the Accept offline (coach_confirmed; transparency without push).
  • Cross-role events — user-as-coach sessions show as muted dashed tiles with a “Coach” tag → read-only drawer + Switch to coach.
  • Grid — full 00:00–24:00, auto-scrolled to the now-line on open. No off-hours shading here: an athlete has no availability to violate, so there is nothing to grey out — the wash zones (.fit-cal-offhours) belong to the coach calendar and to booking grids, where the counterparty’s hours constrain the choice.

Statuses + pills are canonical (fit-ui.css .fit-cal-event.request/.awaiting/.missed/.finished + .fit-cal-event-pill--*) — shared verbatim with the coach calendar. Spec: event-statuses.md (both roles) + booking-flow.md.

Semi-private (proposal, specs/semi-private.md). A coach can put a second person on a personal session. Two states of this same drawer cover the athlete side:

  • Session updated · re-confirm — the anchor athlete is asked again, with was → now for both composition and price. Declining is not a cancellation: the session reverts to the terms she booked, which is why the secondary action reads Keep as it was and is not styled destructive.
  • Invited to join someone — the ordinary request state plus an inline note naming who is already on the session, so nobody accepts a "personal" booking and finds a stranger on the court.
  • Money is per seat and private. The roster is written from the reader's side — "You" is whoever is looking — and the other row carries no price at all. Once seats carry money, the event-level price row is hidden: one number at the top would be a different person's.
