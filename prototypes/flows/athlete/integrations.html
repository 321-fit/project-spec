Integrations

Two independent connections (Settings → Integrations). Each is its own card with its own state — neither depends on the other.

  • Strava — Connect → OAuth (opens the Strava app / Safari). Connected → shows @username + Disconnect. Backend: strava_connection table already shipped (2026-06-22). Posts a finished session to Strava (Upload API).
  • Apple Health — Enable → the native iOS Health permission dialog (HKHealthStore.requestAuthorization(), not OAuth — represented here as a mock). After each session the app reads the workout metrics (heart rate, active energy, distance, pace) and sends them to the backend. There's no in-app disconnect — access is revoked in iOS Settings → Privacy → Health; we show that hint.

How they combine (info card at the bottom):

  • Strava only → posts a manual activity (no metrics).
  • Health only → collects your metrics, stored with us, nothing posted out.
  • Both → posts to Strava via Upload API with your metrics — heart rate, calories, distance (TCX file).

Cross-platform: Apple Health is iOS-only — Android uses Health Connect (same card, same Enable → system permission flow, reads the same metrics). Strava is identical on both.

Where the sensor data lands on the backend (workout-metrics ingest endpoint + storage) — open, to design next per Yuri.
